Why YPF exists

Faith that has somewhere to live.

YPF brings young adults in Ikola into a community where faith, work, relationships, growth and service can be discussed honestly and lived intentionally.

We want young professionals who are rooted in Christ, excellent in their work, generous with their lives, and connected enough to help one another become who God is calling them to be.
