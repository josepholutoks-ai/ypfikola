Ikola, Lagos

A home for young professionals growing in faith, together.

Young Professionals Forum brings young adults across Ikola into one community — for fellowship, mentorship, and purposeful living, on and off the job.

See our groups

Why we gather

We're a community of young adults navigating early careers and everyday life — holding on to faith while we build.

Our Mission

To raise young professionals in Ikola who reflect Christ in their careers, relationships, and communities — equipped, accountable, and unashamed of their faith.

Our Vision

A thriving network of purpose-driven young adults in Ikola and beyond, known for excellence at work and depth in faith.

What you get as a member

Three things we're building the Forum around.

01

Community

A network of young professionals in Ikola to do life with — not just Sundays, but through the week.

02

Growth

Mentorship, skills sessions, and career conversations that help you grow at work, not just in church.

03

Purpose

Faith and career held together — excellence at work as an expression of what we believe, not separate from it.

Small groups & departments

Every member finds a place to serve and grow. Here's where you can plug in.

Media & Publicity

Tells the Forum's story — photography, social media, livestreams, and design for every event.

Prayer Band

Covers the Forum in prayer, leads intercession before gatherings, and supports members through personal seasons.

Choir & Music

Leads worship at every service and rehearses weekly — open to singers and instrumentalists alike.

Welfare

Looks out for members' wellbeing — check-ins, hospital visits, and support during hard times.

Ushering & Protocol

Sets the tone for every gathering — welcoming guests and keeping services running smoothly.

Outreach & Evangelism

Takes the Forum beyond Ikola — community outreach, campus visits, and sharing the faith.
